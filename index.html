GOOD QUESTIONS

Clarity looks good on everyone.

Does the demo cut a real video?

No. The demo explores an illustrative clip review workflow. Video ingestion, transcription, editing and rendering require production services.

Do you own the content I upload?

No ownership transfer is intended. Production terms must specify limited processing permission for customer-provided media and deletion rules.

Will the system auto-post for me?

Not in the initial plan. Any external publishing requires explicit connection and approval.

How do you know I have the rights to my recording?

You tell us, in a structured attestation for every source: what it is, when it was recorded, your rights basis, every guest or third party in it, and the music or other third-party material it contains, with the basis for each. Processing does not start until that attestation is complete. We do not certify your rights claim, and we cannot detect hidden rights problems — if a source looks wrong, we return it unprocessed rather than guess.

Can I send you a link to a video instead of uploading?

Not as a way around the rights gate. ClipTavera never scrapes or rips third-party content. A link is only ever used where it points to your own recording and a platform-permitted method can retrieve it — the same attestation still applies.

Are finished clips included at every tier?

Clip drafts, hooks and captions are the core deliverable at every tier. Rendered vertical-clip drafts are included on Studio and Team within a published monthly quota that is set from measured production cost before payments open.
